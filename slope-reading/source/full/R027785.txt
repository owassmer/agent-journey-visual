1	# Model extensions for the Akoustis and Charles & Colvard cases (spec)
2	
3	**Status:** proposed, for Owen's review. Written 25 Sep 2026.
4	
5	**Scope:** every change to the model, the contracts, the engine, the agent and the page needed to move the demo from ChromaDex to:
6	- **Akoustis Technologies**, decision date (D) 20 Jun 2024, as the lead case (Owen's choice, 25 Sep 2026);
7	- **Charles & Colvard**, D 18 Nov 2024, as the second case.
8	
9	**How decision dates are set:**
10	- D falls just after the filing that brings the public record up to date. It is justified by that filing, never by when the known outcome lands.
11	- The dispute is live, with its next steps inside the 180-day horizon.
12	- The borrower looks stressed but fundable on bank data alone.
13	- The event's cash reaches Slope's exposure while it is outstanding.
14	
15	**Why Akoustis at 20 June rather than 9 October:**
16	- Uncertainty is at its widest after the verdict. On 20 June the post-trial motions, the injunction, fees and interest are all pending.
17	- Bank data looks fundable: a $9.3M share sale closed in May.
18	- The research's warning comes about six months before the 16 Dec 2024 petition, which falls inside the horizon (it ends 17 Dec).
19	- The cost: a single 90-day draw would be repaid by September, before anything happens. So the loan is modelled as Slope's reusable line (§2.1), whose later draws are outstanding when the petition lands.
20	
21	**Governing documents:** where this spec differs from `Slope_Credit_Scenario_Build_Specification.md`, this spec governs for these changes. The alignment document still governs intent.
22	
23	## 0. What does not change
24	
25	- **Jev** reads the present state and forecasts defined future events. It never sets an amount, a date or a path.
26	- **Code** owns every window, amount range and date draw, and multiplies conditional probabilities along each path. Every structurally feasible path is kept, and stress is a separate view.
27	- **Only impossibility changes structure:** an event that has already occurred, or a payment. Every other finding is evidence.
28	- **Unknown is a typed state, never zero.** Contractual, path-conditioned and probability-weighted series stay distinct.
29	- **Operating draws:** bank-only and event-adjusted views share them.
30	- **Isolation:** unchanged. Sources dated after D are `outcome` sources and reach only the reveal.
