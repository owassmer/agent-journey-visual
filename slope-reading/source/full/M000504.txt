**Deviations from the spec (all stated in the PR)**
- **Headroom:** I used available − need − everything owed (the installment plus any arrears), not the installment alone. Slope debits the whole amount owed, so negative headroom then means exactly that the rule collects less than is owed.
- **Need:** it is measured on gross operating flows, including invoices Slope may later pay, because which future invoices get routed isn't known on the day.
- **Outstanding principal:** it is amount funded minus collections × funded / contractual. Every draw carries the same fee, so this is exact when the contract is fully collected.
- **Page field names:** `full_collection_by_maturity_p` and `uncollected_maturity_cents` stay in the payload so the current page doesn't break, but they now read at the horizon, since a line has no single maturity.
- **Stress view:** it adds a petition placed on each path's peak-exposure day, using a small `with_petition` helper. Nothing derives petitions from disputes.
