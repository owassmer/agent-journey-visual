Delivery: decompose first, then acquire, then build\n516|\n517|### 13.1 Why the decomposition comes before any research or data acquisition\n518|\n519|The chains are the specification of what evidence is needed. Acquiring before decomposing gets that order backwards, in four ways:\n520|\n521|1. **Only a decomposed question tells you what to look for.** A holistic question (\"will the court uphold the judgment?\") makes everything and nothing relevant. Asking how the actor decides names the specific things that answer each step:\n522|   - the judge's pace on this docket's earlier motions;\n523|   - the standard for remittitur;\n524|   - what a surety requires as collateral;\n525|   - whether JPMorgan's pledge releases the excess deposit;\n526|   - how many months of head start the trial record supports.\n527|\n528|   The round 2 sweep collected \"everything that could move a forecast\" and still missed every one of these. It also gathered material that no chain uses.\n529|2. **Evidence found first quietly shapes the model.** When acquisition comes first, the model grows branches around the figures that happen to turn up. The \"$305k remittitur\" branch came from a number found in a motion, not from how remittitur works. Decomposing first means the structure follows how decisions are actually made, and evidence then fills a named slot or leaves a typed gap.\n530|3. **Builder research reads outcomes; the design must not.** Our acquisition work is builder-side and sees later events. Chains derived from the law and from each actor's decision process are designed without the outcome. Acquiring targeted pre-D evidence afterwards keeps the design honest. The acquisition notes' \"check against the outcome\" shows exactly the temptation this order avoids.\n531|4. **Gaps become visible and research effort goes where it matters.** Each chain step states its basis and what it needs. If acquisition can't supply it, the gap shows as a typed unknown or an explicit Jev residual, instead of disappearing into a holistic number. Outside-view reference classes (R1) can only be named once the actor decisions are. Their research is prioritised by the steps the lender's outcome depends on.\n532|\n533|**Example:** the remitted amount (Decision D4).
