Measure with `footprint <pid>`, `top -l 1 -pid <pid> -stats mem` or `/usr/bin/time -l`.\n- **Disk:** swap lives on the data volume and can take 3+ GB of free disk during a run, then return it within minutes. `slope analyze` needs about 3 GB free while it writes. Each worktree's `var/dev` (~760 MB) and `var/analysis` (~800 MB) and merged worktrees' `.venv`s are rebuildable; delete them first. `uv cache prune` blocks while a viewer's `uv run` holds the cache lock.
