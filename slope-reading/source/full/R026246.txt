## Overview

Slope is a B2B payments platform for enterprise companies that offers order-to-cash automation using leading AI tools that power checkout, customer and vendor risk assessment, embedded short-term financing, and payment reconciliation.

## Underwriting & Pricing Model

Slope provides automated, 0-question, instantaneous underwriting for B2B installment payments. The company generates revenue through transaction fees on payment volume and recurring fees for its order-to-cash automation platform.

The embedded financing component functions by Slope paying merchants immediately when buyers select net-terms options, then collecting payments from buyers with interest over 30-90 day periods.

## Working Capital Solutions

Slope's embedded finance solution enables enterprise to mid-market companies to receive payments at point of purchase while giving their business buyers the flexibility to defer payment terms via instalments or net terms. Slope's fully automated embedded finance solution and innovative underwriting capabilities are disrupting the B2B payments industry and helping under-served small and medium-sized enterprise (SMEs) access within seconds the capital they need.
