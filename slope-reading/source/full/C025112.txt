Examples: an allegation treated as a liability, a paid matter treated as a future outflow, a noncash gain treated as cash, the wrong entity, a disputed demand treated as a payment. It does not predict risk, and it never sets amounts, dates or decisions.\n\nThe graph (source \u2192 Jev observation \u2192 atomic finding \u2192 effect proposal \u2192 engine result) is the product's audit trail, so it has to be recorded now rather than reconstructed later.\n\n## Unchanged (non-negotiables the pivot reinforces)\n- Jev confidence is never a probability, a weight or a haircut, and a Jev answer never activates a cash adjustment automatically.\n- Dates, arithmetic, double-count protection and cash conservation stay in `app/finance` (merged step 3).\n- Snapshot isolation stays as it is (step 2 `EvidenceStore`).
