## Overview

Slope is a B2B payments platform for enterprise companies offering order-to-cash automation using leading AI tools that power checkout, customer and vendor risk assessment, embedded short-term financing, and payment reconciliation.

## JPMorgan Partnership

J.P. Morgan Payments has chosen Slope to help provide clients access to a short-term financing solution that offers an extension of payment terms to business customers, powered by Slope's platform and leveraging Slope's AI-driven underwriting and J.P. Morgan's debt facility. Slope has raised a total of $252 million, including $77 million in equity funding and $175 million in debt financing, with Sam Altman, CEO of OpenAI, also investing in Slope's latest round of fundraising.

## AI-Driven Underwriting Capabilities

Slope's AI capabilities are a market differentiator, as they use AI not only for initial underwriting but also for ongoing risk monitoring of the portfolio. Slope has launched SlopeAI, which makes its AI underwriting platform available to financial institutions and wholesalers, including access to its LLMs for bank data enrichment (SlopeGPT, TransFormer) and KYB/KYC powered by AI agents.
