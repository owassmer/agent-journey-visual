## Payment Terms

Slope offers buyers 30 and 60-day terms, or 60 and 90-day installments with repayment via card (credit or debit) or ACH. Flexible terms include net terms up to 90 days, plus monthly installments up to 120 days.

## Financing Fees and Rates

Slope offers risk-based pricing, with financing fees as low as 0.99% on Net 30 and 2.99% on Net 90 (equivalent to below 12% APR) for their best customers. Additionally, fees are as low as 1.60% on Net 30, and Slope advertises net terms rates as low as 1.60% for Net 30, with embedded capital APRs from 9.00%.

## Key Features

There are no hidden charges—the cost of borrowing is straightforward with no extra fees for applying or maintaining financing. For early repayment, fees are prorated, so you only get charged for actual days borrowed.

## How It Works for Merchants

Merchants can offer net-30/60/90 and instalment terms to their business customers while Slope pays out immediately and handles underwriting and collections.
