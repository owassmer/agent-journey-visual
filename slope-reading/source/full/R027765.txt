# Acquisition round: Wolfspeed v. Charles & Colvard, D = 18 Nov 2024

Builder-side acquisition, 25 Sep 2026. It collects every public source dated on or before 18 Nov 2024 that bears on an item still unresolved at D. The source texts are in `sources_cthr_20241118/`.

**Scope and isolation:** as for Akoustis (`acquisition_akoustis_20240620.md`). Items dated after D are listed at the end as reveal-only.

Direction: ↑ means the evidence raises the adverse outcome for the borrower; ↓ lowers it; ↔ bears on it without a clear direction.

## 1. Unresolved at D, and what the public record says

### 1.1 The award on each claim head

The arbitration papers are confidential, **but the contract under arbitration is public.** It governs what the arbitrators may award.

|---|---|---|

### 1.2 When the award arrives

| Evidence | Source, date |
|---|---|
| The hearing was held the week of 30 Sep 2024. | NT 10-Q, 15 Nov 2024 |
| **AAA Commercial Rules:** the award is due no later than 30 days after the hearing is closed, unless the parties agree otherwise. The hearing's closing can follow post-hearing briefs. The contract lets the parties vary the rules. | AAA Commercial Rules (public); Supply Agreement ¶16(b) |
| The company is holding back its 10-K (due 30 Sep) and its Q1 10-Q "because of the potential determination of the pending outcome of the confidential arbitration". The company itself expects the outcome soon. | NT 10-K, 1 Oct; NT 10-Q, 15 Nov |

### 1.3 Wolfspeed's security and remedies outside the award

| Evidence | Source, date |
|---|---|
| **Wolfspeed already held a security interest before any settlement.** The supply agreement grants Cree "a security interest in all Collateral" securing amounts due for Products, subordinate to the company's lender except for "Cree Priority Collateral". Cree may act against the priority collateral after a 30-day standstill. | Supply Agreement ¶6(c), 2014 |
| Those lien rights were set up with an intercreditor agreement with Wells Fargo, the lender in 2014. Whether they carry over to JPMorgan, the lender since 2021, is **unverified**. | 8-K Ex. 10.2, 16 Dec 2014 |
| Either party may seek provisional or injunctive relief in court during the arbitration. None has been disclosed. | Supply Agreement ¶16(b) |

### 1.4 The JPMorgan line and its renewal

| Evidence | Source, date |
|---|---|
| The line has $2.3M drawn, is secured by a $5.05M deposit, and **matures 31 Jan 2025** (inside the horizon). | 8-K, 12 Nov 2024, and note Ex. 10.1 |
| Events of default include insolvency, **"any attachment, seizure, sequestration, levy, or garnishment" against any property**, and any material adverse change. Remedies: stop lending, terminate, accelerate, set off. | Credit agreement §7.1–7.2, 8-K Ex. 10.1, 13 Jul 2021 |
| Enforcing an award against the company's property would therefore also be a default under the line. | Inference from the two documents above |

### 1.8 The counterparty

- Wolfspeed's SEC filings never mention "Charles & Colvard" (EDGAR full-text search on Wolfspeed's filings, 2014–2026: zero hits). The claim is immaterial to Wolfspeed.
- Wolfspeed's own financial position before D, which could shape its appetite for a quick cash settlement, was **not researched** in this round.

## 2. What remains truly unavailable at D

- The demand, the briefs, the hearing record and the parties' damages arguments are confidential.

- The balance sheets for 30 Jun and 30 Sep 2024, which were filed after D. The spec's decision D1 still applies. The NT 10-K now gives a pre-D anchor for FY2024 sales ($22.5M).

## 3. Market facts (approved; no price or volume)

- The reverse split and the restored bid-price compliance.
- The S-3 shelf: effective, but likely unusable after the late filing.
- The deposit-secured JPMorgan line and its 31 Jan 2025 maturity.
