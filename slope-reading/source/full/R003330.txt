Matters that are generally known to the public at large or to people in a trade or business, are not trade secrets. Nor can information be considered a trade secret if it would be ascertainable with reasonable ease from publicly available information. In addition, a trade secret must possess enough originality so that it can be distinguished from everyday knowledge. Absolute secrecy is not necessary for information to qualify as a trade secret.
