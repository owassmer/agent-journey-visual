# Slope Product Specifics

## What is Financed
Supplier bills and vendor payments. The platform enables businesses to "pay your bills over time" through flexible financing options.

## Payment Flow
Slope facilitates payments to vendors via ACH or wire transfers (domestic and international), though the exact mechanism of whether Slope pays vendors directly isn't explicitly detailed.

## Terms & Structure
- **Net Terms Option**: Net 30 available
- **Installment Option**: Multiple installments offered
- **Repayment Methods**: Card or ACH payments accepted

## Pricing
- **Fees**: Starting at 1.60% on Net 30 terms
- Card repayments earn reward points
- Fees vary based on "risk assessment and loan term"

## Key Features
- **Early Repayment**: Allowed anytime with fee savings; borrowers can repay partial amounts using different payment methods
- **Approval**: Described as "instant pre approval"
- **Flexibility**: "Choose Your Terms, Pay Your Way" with multiple payment options

## Underwriting
Specific underwriting inputs aren't detailed, but requirements include "minimum revenue and business requirements" and may require "Personal Guaranty."

## Lender
Business loans are "made by Lead Bank" and "subject to credit approval."
