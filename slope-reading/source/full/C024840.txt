A source is admitted only if all of these hold:\n  - its `mission_membership` for that snapshot is `eligible`, or `metadata_only` (catalog row only, no text);\n  - it belongs to the snapshot's case;\n  - its availability is known and at or before the cutoff (date-only records count at end of day, New York time);\n  - its SHA-256 matches the catalog.\n\n  Excluded sources aren't in the database at all, not even their IDs, and a test scans the raw database bytes to confirm it. Reading or filtering by an excluded ID gives the same `not available in this snapshot` error as a nonexistent ID, so the error doesn't reveal that a later document exists. A source marked eligible whose date is after the cutoff stops the build, and so does a hash mismatch.\n- **Original table context is retrievable.** Tables are stored as structured rows with header rows, the caption (the preceding lead-in sentence), up to three preceding paragraphs, the following paragraph, a unit hint, the heading path and the source's availability date. Each table also appears in place within its section text, so reading the section gives the whole passage.
