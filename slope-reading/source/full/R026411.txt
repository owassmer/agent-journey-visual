# Slope Borrowing Limits and Terms

Based on the provided content, here's what Slope discloses about their lending approach:

## Assessment Factors
Slope evaluates "financial performance and credit history to determine a merchant's borrowing power in seconds."

## Four Key Determinations

1. **Pre-approved Limit**: The maximum amount a business is authorized to borrow

2. **Order Limit**: The largest single transaction amount eligible for financing

3. **Terms**: Repayment options vary by merchant partner and include "a variety of net and installment terms"

4. **Pricing**: The financing fee applicable to each term choice

- Fee percentages or pricing structures
