Show the right answer, not the machinery. The front page states one decision with confidence: the recommended offer, one or two conditions phrased as actions (e.g. "fund $150k/6m conditional on Knight's written consent"; "request the July bank statement; if ≥$X of the settlement was paid, $250k is feasible"), and dated cash flows (loan collections beside borrower monthly cash, before/after). Two or three drivers with source click-through. Jev checks, overrides, reconciliations, unknown lists and reviewer objections live only in a collapsed "How we know" drill-down.

The Codex reviewer (step 6) is a quality gate before anything is shown: one repair pass, then the corrected result; unresolved material issue → INCOMPLETE_REVIEW and "not ready", never a hedged decision. The outcome reveal after lock (decision on 13 Aug 2024 vs what happened) is the strongest demo moment.

**Why:** Owen pushed back (2026-09-25) that hedging and disagreement logs make the demo read as AI uncertainty; Russell should see a credit decision.
**How to apply:** steps 5–7 design and wording (spec §4 and step 6 row updated in PR #6). Unknowns are never hidden or zeroed; they become action-phrased conditions. Internal gates (INCOMPLETE_REVIEW on disputes) stay honest; the demo run must resolve disputes, not display them. See [[jev-pivot-decisions]].
