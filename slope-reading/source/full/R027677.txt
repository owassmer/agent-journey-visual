# Nanophase Technologies (NANX, now Solésence Inc., SLSN), CIK 883107: BASF Corp. v. Nanophase

Lane W3 (state commercial courts), 25 Sep 2026. Verified by the W3 worker from EDGAR primary documents fetched with curl (listed below). Builder-side: outcomes read.

## Borrower
- Romeoville/Bolingbrook, IL. Makes zinc-oxide and other mineral ingredients (personal-care ingredients, sold mainly to BASF under a 1999 exclusive Zinc Oxide Supply Agreement) and, through subsidiary Solésence LLC, finished skin-care / SPF products for brands (contract manufacturer to beauty brands). SIC 2844 (cosmetics). OTCQB.
- Revenue: FY2022 $37.3M; FY2023 $37.3M (10-K FY2023: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883107/000199937124004138/nanx-10k_123123.htm).
- Q3 2023: revenue $7.96M, cost of revenue $6.43M, net loss $1.44M; 9M 2023 net loss $2.26M (10-Q Q3 2023: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883107/000199937123000211/nanx-10q_093023.htm).
- Customer concentration (Q3 2023 10-Q, Note 8 / FY2024 Q3 10-Q table): customer #2 "Personal Care Ingredients" (BASF) = 30% of Q3 2023 revenue, 31% of 9M 2023; customer #1 (Solésence) 21%/11%.
- Buys high-purity zinc and other raw materials (some single-source, China/Korea) — 10-K FY2022 "Sources and Availability of Raw Materials".

## Dispute
- **BASF Corp. v. Nanophase Technologies Corp., Docket No. MRS-L-1396-22** (N.J. Super. Ct., Law Div., Morris County), filed 9 Aug 2022. Docket number verified in the filed settlement agreement (8-K 11 Apr 2024, Ex. 99.2: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883107/000117184324001961/exh_992.htm).
- Claims: breach of exclusivity, capacity and inventory provisions of the Zinc Oxide Supply Agreement (Nanophase selling zinc oxide / Solésence products containing it in BASF's "Field"); unjust enrichment; good faith. Relief: **unspecified damages, permanent injunction** against sales to anyone other than BASF of a broad range of zinc-oxide products, fees. Not seeking termination. (10-Q Q3 2022: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883107/000138713122011376/nanx-10q_093022.htm; 8-K 8 Sep 2022: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883107/000138713122009591/nanx-8k_090622.htm)
- Parallel: Nanophase v. BASF declaratory action, Circuit Court of Cook County, filed 7 Sep 2022; dismissed as duplicative 16 Mar 2023.
- Rulings: NJ MTD denied 10 Feb 2023; Nanophase answered + counterclaims 28 Feb 2023 (declaratory judgment; BASF breached 70% requirements-purchase obligation); 6 Oct 2023: BASF MTD denied as to DJ counterclaim, granted as to the purchase-requirement counterclaim; BASF answered 31 Oct 2023; discovery ongoing (10-Q Q3 2023).
- No accrual: "estimated contingent loss was not recorded ... nor is a range of loss estimable" (10-K FY2022, FY2023).
- Litigation spend flagged: 9M 2023 SG&A up "due in large part to increased legal costs" (10-Q Q3 2023 MD&A).

## Proposed D
- **14 Nov 2023** (Q3 2023 10-Q filed; 13 Nov 2023 Strandler bridge-note financing). Dispute live (discovery; counterclaim just pleaded out). Next developments unknown at D.
- Caveat from the later-filed settlement: the parties had signed a binding ZnO Exclusivity MOU on **27 Nov 2023** and supply/modified-product MOUs on **21 Dec 2023** (Ex. 99.2 §2(j)). Not public at the time; public on 11 Apr 2024. So the dispute was de facto resolved ~2 weeks after D.
- Alternative D: 14 Nov 2022 (Q3 2022 10-Q; cash $510k at 30 Sep 2022; MTD pending). Earlier, cash tighter, but outcome ~17 months out (fails 12-month outcome by a margin).

- Cash **$1.19M**; AR $3.20M; inventory $10.12M; AP **$6.27M**; lines of credit, related party **$6.34M** (current); long-term related-party debt $1.0M; total equity $3.93M.
- 9M 2023 operating cash flow +$0.64M; capex $0.85M.
- Facilities: all from affiliates of majority holder Bradford T. Whitmore (Beachcorp LLC, Strandler LLC): A/R Revolver up to $8.0M (qualified AR borrowing base), Inventory Facility up to $4.0M (50% of qualified inventory), Term Loan $1.0M; **prime + 0.75%** (≈9.25% in late 2023); maturity 31 Mar 2024, extended to 31 Mar 2025 on 13 Nov 2023 with inventory facility raised to $5.2M at 55% advance; plus a new **$2.0M Strandler Bridge Note** at prime + 0.75%, due on a planned rights offering or 13 May 2024. A Libertyville Bank & Trust line supports the Bolingbrook lease (undrawn at 31 Dec 2022). (10-K FY2022: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883107/000183988223007887/nanx-10k_123122.htm; 10-Q Q3 2023 Note 6)
- No going-concern doubt; auditor (RSM) made going concern a **critical audit matter** (10-K FY2022, FY2023).
- My estimate (not disclosed): borrowing base at 30 Sep 2023 ≈ AR-based ~$2.5M + inventory ~$5.2M ≈ $7.7M vs $6.34M drawn → ~$1.4M headroom, plus the $2M bridge from 13 Nov 2023. **Unverified estimate.**

## Criterion 8
- (a) **Partial (1).** No third-party bank revolver of size; the working-capital lines are insider-controlled, borrowing-base limited, short-dated, but cheap (~9.25%) and were expanded on 13 Nov 2023. A bank-data view sees a controlling holder topping up at prime+0.75%, which is cheaper than Slope's ~20% APR. Constrained only in the sense that it is discretionary.
- (b) **Pass (2).** Cash $1.19M vs quarterly cash costs ~ $9M (COGS $6.43M + R&D $1.06M + SG&A $1.70M in Q3 2023) → ~2 weeks of gross outflows; AP $6.27M ≈ 1 quarter of COGS.
- (c) **Pass (2).** COGS ~$2.1M/month; raw materials (high-purity zinc etc.) and packaging bought on invoice; AP $6.3M.
- (d) **Weak (0–1).** Exposure unquantified (unspecified damages); the real risk was an injunction on Solésence zinc-oxide sales and loss/repricing of BASF volume (30% of revenue). No dated cash consequence within 180 days was disclosed or occurred.
