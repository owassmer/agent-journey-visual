## Borrower
- Moissanite gemstone and finished-jewelry brand (Morrisville, NC); SIC 3911 jewelry. Online (DTC) and Traditional (wholesale/retail partners) segments.
- FY ended 30 Jun 2024 net sales about $22.5M (NT 10-K / 8-K Item 2.02, 1 Oct 2024: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015155/000110465924104971/tm2425308d2_8k.htm). FY2023 $29.9M.
- Buys SiC crystals (sole supplier Wolfspeed, formerly Cree), gold and precious metals; finished jewelry is assembled by contract "manufacturing partners" in the U.S., China, India, Mexico, Costa Rica, Hong Kong (FY24 10-K, Business).
- Q ended 30 Sep 2024: net cash used in operations $1.29M (10-Q filed 4 Aug 2025: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015155/000114036125028619/ef20047787_10q.htm).

## Dispute (all from company filings)
- 12 Dec 2014 Exclusive Supply Agreement: C&C buys 100% of SiC from Wolfspeed with minimum quarterly quantities; total commitment about $52.95M through June 2025, about $24.75M still unpurchased at 31 Mar 2024; no SiC purchases in the 12 months to 31 Mar 2024 "while engaged in discussions" (Q3 FY24 10-Q, Note 9, filed 6 May 2024: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015155/000114036124024560/ef20026281_10q.htm).
- 13 Jul 2023 AAA demand (settlement agreement recital A); company filings say initiated 28 Jul 2023. Claims: FY2023 minimum-purchase shortfall $4.25M; unpaid delivered crystals $3.30M; anticipatory breach of remaining purchase obligations $18.5M; plus interest, costs, fees (Q3 FY24 10-Q, Legal Proceedings). Press release later called total sought "in excess of $28 million".
- Hearing scheduled 30 Sep 2024 (disclosed in 10-Q of 6 May 2024); held 30 Sep–2 Oct 2024 (settlement recital A).
- NT 10-K, 1 Oct 2024: the 10-K is late because of "the potential determination of the pending outcome of the confidential arbitration" (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015155/000110465924104965/tm2425308d1_nt10k.htm). NT 10-Q, 15 Nov 2024, same reason, and states the company expects to disclose substantial doubt about going concern (https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015155/000110465924119736/tm2428546d1_nt10q.htm).
- 5/6 Dec 2024 interim award: expectation damages ($18.5M) rejected; C&C to pay about $1.3M for product already purchased (already a payable) and buy about $2.0M of consigned raw material; 8% interest from 24 Apr 2023 (later corrected to 12%); AAA fees on C&C; limited attorneys' fees; fee petition due 4 Jan 2025 (8-K 11 Dec 2024: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015155/000110465924127622/tm2430786-1_8k.htm). Recital B: $3,296,974.28 awarded.
- 6 Jan 2025: Wolfspeed fee petition $611,305.78 fees + $102,042.65 costs (recital F).
- 10 Feb 2025 settlement: supply agreement terminated; total $4.77M payable $500k on 10 Feb 2025, $1,834,105 by 28 Feb 2025, $2,437,095.89 by 31 Dec 2025; Wolfspeed gets a **lien on C&C's assets** for the final payment; $100k liquidated damages per confidentiality breach (8-K 14 Feb 2025: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015155/000110465925014250/tm256573d1_8k.htm; agreement filed as Ex. 10.1 to the Q3 FY25 10-Q: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1015155/000114036125028626/ef20051325_ex10-1.htm).
- No court confirmation/vacatur petition found (settlement says the obligation "arises solely out of this Settlement Agreement"); CourtListener search for "Charles & Colvard" returned no such case (API query, 25 Sep 2026).

## Criterion 8 at D
- (a) PARTIAL. No uncollateralized credit; the JPMC line only releases the company's own pledged deposit and is renewed quarter-to-quarter with a MAC clause. But $2.7M of the $5M line was nominally undrawn at SOFR+1.35%, and the company never drew it even when cash fell to $1.5M (reason not disclosed — unverified whether JPMC would have funded). Equity route impaired (expired shelf, Nasdaq deficiencies, late filings).
- (b) PASS. Going concern (Q3 FY24 10-Q; NT 10-Q 15 Nov 2024). Unrestricted cash about $2.7M at 30 Sep 2024 against about $1.3M quarterly net burn and gross outflows around $2M/month (estimate from sales ≈ $5M/qtr + burn) → roughly 1–1.5 months of gross outflows, about two quarters of net burn. Stressed, not collapsed (no default).
- (c) PASS (estimated). Recurring purchases of gold/metals and contract jewelry assembly; AP $7.4–8.6M; FY24 AP rose $3.49M (stretched vendors). Monthly purchases estimated $0.5–1.5M (inferred, not disclosed per invoice).
- (d) PASS. Claimed exposure $26M+ vs. $2.7M cash; even the realised $4.77M = 1.7× unrestricted cash, $2.33M due within 18 days of settlement, and the award also forced the 10-K/10-Q delays that led to delisting.
