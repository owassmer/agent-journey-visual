## Case Overview
A contract product liability case was filed on November 10, 2022 in the California Central District Court under case number 2:2022cv08222.

## Background and Complaint
On November 4, 2022, Barfresh received notification from Schreiber Foods, Inc. that it was denying any responsibility for the defective manufacture of certain products, after Barfresh had been experiencing performance and quality issues with products manufactured by Schreiber under the supply agreement. In response to Schreiber's denial of any liability, on November 10, 2022, Barfresh filed a complaint in the United States District Court for the Central District of California, Western Division, against Schreiber claiming a breach of the supply agreement and seeking economic damages.

## Case Status
On January 20, 2023, Barfresh filed a voluntary dismissal of the complaint which allows the parties to reach a potential resolution outside of the court system; however, Barfresh had the right to refile the complaint in California State Court. As of August 2024, Barfresh received offers to fund the lawsuit on a non-recourse basis and had spent approximately $300,000 over the past two years for this lawsuit.
