**Defects, each confirmed with a probe on the Akoustis fixture (2,128 paths, 66 Jev nodes):**
1. **A stay motion drops every levy before the stay is approved.** When the stay is "yes", the model never asks about early registration or enforcement (`forecast.py:512-516` before the ruling, `:587-595` after it). None of the 220 early stay-yes paths has an early-registration step, and none of the 400 post-ruling stay-yes paths has an enforcement step. Yet the early-registration order comes before stay approval on 44.7% of trajectories, when median cash is $10.3M. After the ruling the figure is 13.3%. These paths are possible and change cash, so removing them breaks the rule that only impossibility removes a path.
2. **The "beyond" amount class is not cash-identical, as the PR claims.** It groups 32 ruling outcomes and prices them all at the smallest total, $30.1M (`forecast.py:284-300`). 26 of those outcomes are increases up to $112.9M, which under the code's own rule wait 30 extra days (`events.py:448-449`). That shifts several post-ruling dates, including the second notes-default date. Jev is also told about $30.1M is owed on paths where the award was trebled to $112.9M.
3. **The amended-judgment rule runs the sensitivity as the base, and the sensitivity crashes.** The contract base says only the increase waits 30 days; the code delays the whole amount (`events.py:449`). Turning on the sensitivity raises `ValueError` because the code tries to read the string `'whole_amount'` as a number. Fixing this also fixes most of item 2.
4. **Legal fees keep running after a vacatur.** The contract says spend stops on payment, settlement, vacatur or petition (`dispute_model.json:438-441`). The vacated ruling never stops it (`events.py:442-444`), so $0.304M per trajectory is still spent after the ruling. The same ruling group also holds new-trial outcomes, where the case continues and spend should not stop.
5. **The injunction question (J7) gets the wrong legal standard.** It cites Rule 62(b), the bond-stay rule (`dispute_model.json:711-713`). The design calls for eBay, the federal trade-secret statute, the North Carolina statute (§66-154(a)) and Rule 62(c). The two settlement questions also cite Rule 62(b), which has no bearing on them.
6. **Minor:** 22 of the 26 forecast questions sent to Jev are ungrammatical ("Does the court grants…").
