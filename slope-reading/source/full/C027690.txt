Written 25 Sep 2026.\n\n**Scope:** every change to the model, the contracts, the engine, the agent and the page needed to move the demo from ChromaDex to:\n- **Akoustis Technologies**, decision date (D) 9 Oct 2024, as the lead case;\n- **Charles & Colvard**, D 18 Nov 2024, as the second case.\n\n**Governing documents:** where this spec differs from `Slope_Credit_Scenario_Build_Specification.md`, this spec governs for these changes. The alignment document still governs intent.\n\n## 0. What does not change\n\n- **Jev** reads the present state and forecasts defined future events. It never sets an amount, a date or a path.\n- **Code** owns every window, amount range and date draw, and multiplies conditional probabilities along each path. Every structurally feasible path is kept, and stress is a separate view.\n- **Only impossibility changes structure:** an event that has already occurred, or a payment. Every other finding is evidence.\n- **Unknown is a typed state, never zero.** Contractual, path-conditioned and probability-weighted series stay distinct.\n- **Operating draws:** bank-only and event-adjusted views share them.\n- **Isolation:** unchanged. Sources dated after D are `outcome` sources and reach only the reveal.\n\n---\n\n## 1. The cases\n\n### 1.1 Akoustis Technologies (AKTS, CIK 1584754), D = 9 Oct 2024\n\n**Dispute:** *Qorvo, Inc. v. The Slope loan (run inputs and engine)\n\n### 2.1 Sizing the loan (run inputs; no policy engine)\n\n`cases/<case>/make_run_inputs.py` applies Slope's published rule to the bank feed and writes `financing_plan.supplied_terms`. The module still takes terms as inputs; the case script shows how they were reconstructed. Label, once: \"Terms reconstructed by applying Slope's published sizing rule to the connected-bank baseline.\"\n\n**Monthly revenue after debt service:** mean monthly customer receipts over the last three complete months, minus mean monthly debt service over the same months. Debt service is the categories tagged `debt_service` in the feed.\n\n**Line:** 15% of that figure. That's the low end of Slope's 15\u201333% range, the end Slope uses for a stressed profile.\n\n**Draw:** the largest single supplier invoice (categories tagged `supplier_invoice`) in the last three months that is no larger than the line.\n\n**Terms:** 3 equal monthly installments at 30, 60 and 90 days; fee of 3.7%.\n\n**Funding:** the next business day after D.\n\n**Expected figures:**\n\n| | Akoustis | Charles & Colvard |\n|---|---|---|\n| Monthly revenue after debt service | \u2248 $2.28M \u2212 $0.22M | \u2248 $1.88M \u2212 $0.01M |\n| Line | \u2248 $0.31M (above Slope's $250k automatic approval, so manual review; noted once) | \u2248 $0.28M |\n| Draw | one Tai-Saw or fab-materials invoice | one gold or contract-assembly invoice |\n\nThe page's amount control keeps its range (0 up to the line).\n\n### 2.2 The collection rule\n\nReplace \"take the lesser of what is owed and available cash\" with **cash above the draw's own operating need**.\n\nFor trajectory *d* on day *t*:\n- `need[d,t] = max(0, \u2212min over s in (t, t+30] of (cumops[d,s] \u2212 cumops[d,t]))`. This is the lowest point of that draw's own operating flows over the next 30 days.\n- On each due date, and at each month-end while anything is overdue:\n  - `capacity = max(0, available[d,t] \u2212 need[d,t])`;\n  - `collect = min(owed, capacity)`.\n- Anything unpaid stays owed and is caught up later on the same rule.\n\nThe rationale is stated once on the page: a borrower near its operating floor protects payroll and suppliers, and an ACH debit fails when the funds have been moved.\n\n### 2.3 Insolvency proceedings (new; used by the reorganisation node in \u00a73.4)\n\nWhen a path carries a petition date `p`:\n- **Collections stop from `p`** (11 U.S.C. \u00a7362, the automatic stay).\n- **Outstanding at `p`** is reported as a **stayed claim**, whose recovery is unknown and outside the horizon. It is a typed state. It is not collected and is never shown as zero loss.\n- **Preference exposure:** collections dated in `[p \u2212 90 days, p)` (11 U.S.C. \u00a7547(b)(4)(A)). They are reported as a separate series, not deducted from collections: whether they are clawed back is a legal question the model doesn't answer.\n- **Borrower operating flows continue** after the petition (a debtor in possession keeps operating). Event cash for the dispute stops at `p`: enforcement is stayed.\n\n### 2.4 Lender outputs (added to the views, the scenarios and the CSVs)\n\n- **Payment headroom at each due date:** `available \u2212 need \u2212 installment`, as a distribution (P5, P50, P95) with the probability that it is negative.\n- **Backup liquidity:** `available cash + undrawn availability on the borrower's facilities` (from \u00a74), per day, as an expectation and a P5.\n- **Stayed claim at the horizon, and the probability of a petition before maturity.**\n- **Preference-exposed collections:** expectation, and the probability that any exist.\n- **Line limit Slope's own rule would set** at each installment date, from each trajectory's trailing three months of simulated receipts. This shows when the bank data alone would shrink the line, against when the research flagged the risk. **Decision D3:** include it (recommended; it measures the signal's lead time).\n- **Kept:** collections, timing, outstanding balance, PV at the discount rate, principal dollar-days, and the uncollected balance at maturity and at the horizon.\n\n---\n\n## 3. Dispute model 4.0.0 (`dispute_model.json`)\n\n### 3.1 Forum\n\n`DisputeInstance.forum: Literal[\"court\", \"arbitration\"]`, supplied by the agent.\n- It is checked in code: for `arbitration`, the cited quotes must contain \"arbitration\" or \"arbitrator\"; for `court`, the order reference must match the docket pattern.\n- The forum selects the procedure (\u00a73.2 or \u00a73.3). The payer, the stage and the amounts are still read by Jev and checked by code, as now.\n\n### 3.2 Court procedure: post-trial stage (Akoustis)\n\n**New readings events:**\n- `post_trial_motions_pending`: \"a timely motion for judgment as a matter of law, a new trial, or to alter or amend the judgment on this obligation is pending\".\n- `post_trial_decided`: \"the court has decided the post-trial motions on this obligation\".\n\n**Stage rules, in priority order:**\n1. `paid`\n2. `stay_secured` \u2192 `appeal_pending`\n3. `post_trial_decided` \u2192 `judgment_entered`, with its date as the judgment date\n4. `post_trial_motions_pending` \u2192 **`post_trial`**\n5. `judgment_entered`\n6. `amount_fixed`\n7. `entitlement_decided` \u2192 `amount_pending`\n\n**New rule:** `post_trial_tolling`. Fed. R. App. P. 4(a)(4)(A): the time to appeal runs from the order disposing of the last post-trial motion. At the `post_trial` stage, the judgment date for every later window is therefore the drawn date of the post-trial ruling, not the earlier entry.\n\n**New node `post_trial_ruling`:**\n- Stage `post_trial`; a Choice asked in the window from review to horizon.\n- Options:\n  - `upheld`: \"the court denies the motions and enters final judgment on this obligation\";\n  - `vacated`: \"the court grants judgment as a matter of law or a new trial, vacating this obligation\";\n  - `none`: \"no ruling within the window\".\n- Context factors: `amount_finality`, `settlement_signals`.\n\n**Paths at `post_trial`:**\n- `settle_before_ruling` = yes \u2192 `settled` (existing node and window).\n- Otherwise `post_trial_ruling`:\n  - `upheld` \u2192 the judgment subtree (existing), with the judgment date drawn in the ruling window.\n  - `vacated` \u2192 outcome **`vacated`**: no cash in the horizon.\n  - `none` \u2192 `unresolved`.\n\n**Amount:** the judgment amount is the documented total the agent quotes (verdict plus fee and interest awards; each component must be quoted). The existing post-judgment interest rule applies unless the quote shows interest is included.\n\n### 3.3 Arbitration procedure (Charles & Colvard)\n\n**New readings events:**\n- `arbitration_hearing_held`: \"the arbitration hearing on this obligation has been held\";\n- `award_issued`: \"the arbitrator has issued an award (interim or final) on this obligation\";\n- `award_confirmed`: \"a court has confirmed the award\".\n\n**Stage rules (arbitration):**\n1. `paid`\n2. `award_confirmed` \u2192 `judgment_entered` (the court procedure from there)\n3. `award_issued` \u2192 `award_issued`\n4. `arbitration_hearing_held` \u2192 `award_pending`\n5. no established event \u2192 `claims_pending`, with the request to establish the hearing date.\n\n**Claim heads:**\n- The agent supplies `claim_heads: [{head_id, label, amount_cents}]`, 1\u20134 heads. Each amount must be quoted in the cited findings (the existing `_cents_in_text` check).\n- For a single-amount dispute, the heads are `[the amount]`.\n\n**New nodes:**\n- **`settle_before_award`:** stage `award_pending`; reuses `forecast_settlement`; window from review to review + `settlement_window_days`.\n- **`award_timing`:** Noul, \"the arbitrator issues the award within the window\"; window from review to horizon. no \u2192 `unresolved`.\n- **`head_awarded`**, one node per head:\n  - Noul, \"the award grants this claim head (in whole or substantial part)\".\n  - Asked by the chain rule: head *k* is asked under the assumptions of heads 1..*k*\u22121's branches.\n  - Heads are asked in the agent's order. Joint probability = \u220f P(head *k* | earlier heads). For 3 heads that is 7 nodes.\n  - Context factors: `amount_finality`, `debtor_liquidity` (counterparty-pays only), and `settlement_signals`.\n- **`challenge_award`:**\n  - Noul, \"the payer petitions a court to vacate or modify the award\".\n  - Window: award date to award date + `vacatur_notice_days` (90; 9 U.S.C. \u00a712).\n  - Like `appeal`, but with no secured-stay node: an unconfirmed award cannot be executed.\n  - yes \u2192 the path joins `settle_during_appeal` (reused as \"settle while the challenge is pending\"); if there is no settlement, outcome **`award_challenged`**, with no cash in the horizon.\n- **Payment after an unchallenged award:**\n  - `voluntary_payment` (existing), in the window award date + `award_payment_days` (30).\n  - If unpaid: **`confirmation_enforcement`**, \"the payee confirms the award in court and collects it\", in the window award date + `confirmation_enforcement_days` (120).\n  - Outcomes `paid` and `collected`, then the reorganisation node (\u00a73.4).\n\n**Awarded amount:** \u03a3 over awarded heads of `claimed amount \u00d7 awarded_share`.\n- `awarded_share` is drawn once per head in [`awarded_share_bps.lower`, `upper`]. The default is [10000, 10000] (the head is granted in full), with the sensitivity at [5000, 10000].\n- **Decision D4:** full heads plus the sensitivity (recommended), or a default range.\n- Pre-award interest: new rule `award_interest_bps_per_year` [800, 1200], a statutory or contractual rate range. It is labelled as a model parameter unless the agent quotes the contract rate.\n\n### 3.4 Reorganisation (borrower as payer, both procedures)\n\n**New node `reorganization`:**\n- Noul: \"the payer files a petition under Chapter 11 (or an involuntary petition is filed against it)\".\n- **Asked only when the borrower is the payer.** A counterparty's insolvency is already the existing `uncollected` outcome.\n- **Where it sits:** wherever the judgment or award becomes enforceable against the borrower and is not stayed. That covers:\n  - `no_appeal` or `appeal_unsecured` after `voluntary_payment` = no (court);\n  - no challenge and unpaid after the award (arbitration);\n  - an acceleration context from the financing channel (\u00a74.4).\n- **Window:** from the judgment date (or award date) to the end of the enforcement window. In the acceleration context: from the acceleration date + 1 to acceleration + 30.\n- **Branches:**\n  - yes \u2192 outcome **`reorganization`**, dated inside the window. This triggers \u00a72.3 in the engine.\n  - no \u2192 the existing `enforcement` node.\n- **Context factors:** `debtor_liquidity` is not asked for the borrower. The forecast gets `payer_available_cash` from the bank data, as now, plus the financing instruments' readings (\u00a74.2).\n\n**New outcomes:**\n- `reorganization`: \"A petition on its drawn date: enforcement of this obligation stops, and Slope's collections stop (automatic stay).\"\n- `vacated`\n- `award_challenged`\n\n### 3.5 Settlement timing\n\n**New rule `settlement_upfront_share_bps`** [5000, 10000]:\n- The share of a settlement paid on its drawn date.\n- The remainder is due at settlement + 300 days (usually past the horizon, so outside the period).\n- It replaces the lump sum. The sensitivity shows the lump sum (10000).\n\n### 3.6 Litigation spend (a present factor, not a new tool)\n\n**New factor `litigation_spend_attributed`:**\n- Kind `present`, aggregate `any`.\n- Relevance: \"Does the quoted text attribute the borrower's legal expense to this dispute?\"\n\n**Code rule:** when the factor is established for any live dispute:\n- the bank feed's `legal_fees` category in the operating draws is scaled to zero from the day after the last linked dispute's path resolves within the horizon (`settled`, `paid`, `collected`, `vacated`, `reorganization`);\n- it continues unchanged while any path is pending (appeal, challenge, unresolved).\n\nIt is applied per joint path, on the same operating draws, so both views stay paired. The rule is labelled once.\n\n### 3.7 Operational restrictions (injunction): shown, not in cash, unless quantified\n\n**New readings event:**\n- `injunction_entered`: \"the court has entered an injunction restricting the payer's sales or operations\".\n\n**New node `injunction`:**\n- Noul, \"the court enters the requested injunction\", at the `post_trial` stage.\n- Its cash effect needs a quoted revenue share or amount affected, supplied by the agent as `injunction_effect` and checked in the quotes.\n- **Without one,** the node is forecast and shown, with its cash effect stated as unquantified, together with an evidence request.\n- **Akoustis:** the 10-K says its current products are unaffected. The node is forecast and shown, and no cash effect is invented.\n\n### 3.8 Contract housekeeping\n\n- The `design` text still says \"an established waiver ... constrains the graph\". Correct it.\n- `model_version` becomes 4.0.0.\n\n---\n\n## 4. The financing channel (new, generic)\n\nBoth cases have a financing instrument whose terms turn the dispute into a lender event:\n- **Akoustis:** $44M of 6% convertible notes. They default on a final judgment above $10.0M that stays undischarged, unpaid or unstayed for 60 days after the 30-day appeal period, and on delisting.\n- **Charles & Colvard:** a $5M JPMorgan line, $2.3M drawn. It is secured by a $5.05M deposit, renewed three months at a time (next on 31 Jan 2025), and has a material-adverse-change default.\n\n### 4.1 Domain and the agent's tool\n\nNew domain object `FinancingInstrument` (run collection `financing`):\n\n| Field | Meaning |\n|---|---|\n| `instrument_id`, `dependency_id` | as for disputes |\n| `lender` | the lender or holders |\n| `kind` | `revolver` / `term_or_notes` |\n| `finding_ids` | the cited findings |\n| `outstanding_cents` | amount outstanding; quoted |\n| `limit_cents` | revolvers only; quoted |\n| `collateral_cents` | optional; quoted |\n| `maturity` or `renewal_date` | quoted |\n| `thresholds` | `[{trigger, amount_cents, days}]`; each figure quoted |\n| `linked_disputes` | the disputes whose paths can trigger it |\n\nNew agent tool **`instantiate_financing`**:\n- Same guards as `instantiate_dispute`: accepted findings only; every figure and date checked against the quotes; a neutral reference; no pre-answering prose.\n- Added to the MCP allowlist in `agent_config.json`.\n\n### 4.2 Jev: present-state interpretation (profile `financing_interpretation`)\n\nOne request per cited finding, hydrated as for disputes. Noul questions:\n- `fin_default_unstayed_judgment`: \"The quoted text makes a judgment or award against the borrower above a stated amount, left unpaid or unstayed for a stated period, an event of default under this instrument.\"\n- `fin_default_judgment_entry`: the same trigger on entry alone.\n- `fin_default_settlement`: the same trigger for a settlement agreement above a stated amount.\n- `fin_default_delisting`: the same trigger for the borrower's stock ceasing to be listed.\n- `fin_discretionary_default`: \"The lender may declare a default for a material adverse change, or on its own judgment of the prospect of repayment.\"\n- `fin_lender_may_accelerate`: \"The lender may declare the amount outstanding immediately due on an event of default.\"\n- `fin_lender_may_stop_lending`: revolvers only.\n- `fin_short_dated_renewal`: \"The facility is renewed at the lender's discretion for short periods.\"\n\nAggregation is `any`; established at \u2265 0.5, as now. **A trigger enters the code rules only if it is established and its figure is quoted.**\n\n### 4.3 Code: the default date for each joint path\n\nFor each instrument and joint dispute path, the event-of-default date `e` is the earliest of these, in horizon days, for disputes where the borrower pays:\n- **Unstayed judgment:** the judgment or award date + appeal days (30, or 90 for an arbitration challenge window) + the quoted grace days. This applies when the path leaves the obligation unpaid, not settled and not stayed through that date, and the amount is above the quoted threshold.\n- **Judgment entry:** the judgment date, if the amount is above the threshold.\n- **Settlement:** the settlement's drawn date, if the settlement amount is above the threshold.\n- **Delisting:** from the `delisting` node (\u00a74.4), if established and forecast.\n\nNo trigger \u2192 no default on that path.\n\nA `reorganization` dated before `e` pre-empts the default. The node order does this structurally: reorganisation is asked first where it applies.\n\n### 4.4 Jev: forecasts (profile `financing_forecast`)\n\nAsked per instrument and per trigger context. Node keys follow the pattern `fin:<id>:<node>|cause=<trigger>`.\n\n- **`default_waiver`:** Noul, \"the lender or holders waive the default or agree to forbear\". Window: `e` to `e` + 30.\n  - yes \u2192 nothing further.\n  - no, for `term_or_notes` \u2192 **acceleration** on `e` + 30. The outstanding amount becomes due, and `reorganization|context=acceleration` is asked (\u00a73.4).\n    - yes \u2192 petition.\n    - no \u2192 the borrower pays the outstanding amount (event cash \u2212outstanding). A resulting shortfall shows as negative cash, never as funding from nowhere.\n  - no, for `revolver` \u2192 availability ends at `e` + 30 and the lender may demand the drawn balance. If the facility is cash-collateralised, the drawn balance is repaid from the collateral and the excess collateral is released to available cash on that date.\n- **`facility_renewal`:** only when `fin_short_dated_renewal` is established. Noul, \"the lender renews the facility at its next renewal date\". Window: the quoted renewal date \u00b1 15 days.\n  - no \u2192 the drawn balance is repaid from the collateral on the renewal date, and the excess is released. Availability ends.\n  - It is asked in two contexts: the dispute outcome class so far (`counterparty_receives` / `no_cash`) as of the renewal date.\n- **`delisting`:** optional; only when `fin_default_delisting` is established. Noul, \"the stock ceases to be listed\". Window: review to horizon.\n\n### 4.5 Composition\n\nThe joint paths become (dispute paths) \u00d7 (financing branches conditioned on each dispute path's default date and cause). The probability is the product of the conditional node probabilities, as now.\n\n**Size:** Akoustis has one dispute and one instrument; Charles & Colvard has one dispute and one facility. Both stay in the low hundreds of joint paths, and all are simulated.\n\n---\n\n## 5. Engine changes (`app/analysis`)\n\n- **`events.py`:**\n  - new outcomes (`vacated`, `award_challenged`, `reorganization`);\n  - claim-head amounts;\n  - staged settlements;\n  - post-trial ruling dates driving the judgment windows;\n  - arbitration windows;\n  - financing cash (acceleration, collateral release, repayment);\n  - the petition date per trajectory.\n\n  **Draw keys** follow the existing pattern `(dispute or instrument, node, purpose)`, so a changed probability never moves a date.\n- **`operating.py`:** also return the per-category arrays needed for `legal_fees` and for `need[d,t]`.\n- **`engine.py`:**\n  - the operating-need collection rule (\u00a72.2);\n  - the petition stop, stayed claim and preference series (\u00a72.3);\n  - headroom, backup liquidity and the bank-rule line limit (\u00a72.4).\n- **`core.py`:**\n  - drop `parameter_sensitivity` (the page's controls replace it);\n  - add **signal attribution** (\u00a76).\n- **`build.py`:** financing metadata, claim-head metadata, and the new CSV columns (preference-exposed, stayed, headroom, backup liquidity).\n\n---\n\n## 6. Signal attribution and the recall replay\n\n**Signal attribution**, three rows for every lender metric:\n1. **Bank data only.**\n2. **Plus Jev's readings.** Jev's present-state readings set the structure (who pays, the stage, the heads, the triggers), with neutral forecasts: Noul 0.5 and Choice uniform.\n3. **Plus Jev's forecasts.**\n\nThese are three reweightings of the same trajectories, so no re-simulation is needed.\n\n**Recall replay:** `slope analyze --run <id> --roles` re-asks every forecast with the parties' names replaced by roles. It records the change in each node and the change in the lender metrics in `analysis.json`, under `recall_check`. It runs once per recorded run.\n\n---\n\n## 7. Question registry 4.0.0 and `agent_config`\n\n- **`dispute_interpretation` adds:**\n  - `event_post_trial_motions_pending`, `event_post_trial_decided`;\n  - `event_arbitration_hearing_held`, `event_award_issued`, `event_award_confirmed`;\n  - `event_injunction_entered`;\n  - `bears_on_litigation_spend_attributed`.\n- **`dispute_forecast` adds:**\n  - `forecast_post_trial_ruling` (Choice), `forecast_injunction`;\n  - `forecast_award_timing`, `forecast_head_awarded`, `forecast_challenge_award`, `forecast_confirmation_enforcement`;\n  - `forecast_reorganization`.\n- **New profiles:**\n  - `financing_interpretation`: the eight `fin_*` questions in \u00a74.2;\n  - `financing_forecast`: `forecast_default_waiver`, `forecast_facility_renewal`, `forecast_delisting`.\n- **All forecast prompts** keep the rule that Jev must not use remembered facts about the parties.\n- **`agent_config`:**\n  - the `instantiate_financing` tool;\n  - the new profiles;\n  - Jev budgets reviewed.
