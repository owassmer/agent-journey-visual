{"file_path": "/Users/owenwassmer/.claude/projects/-Users-owenwassmer-dev-Slope-Sparse-Events/memory/forecast-decomposition.md", "content": "---\nname: forecast-decomposition\ndescription: How to break down a Jev forecast \u2014 start from the lender's question, ask how each actor decides until nothing is answerable from law, record, data or arithmetic; every causal link needs a stated basis\nmetadata:\n  type: feedback\n---\n\nBreak a forecast down starting from the lender's question: what decides Slope's collections within the horizon? Follow only the chains that bear on it.\n\nWithin a chain, keep asking how the actor actually decides (court, payer, payee, lender, surety) and what that turns on. Answer each step from the law, the record, data or arithmetic. Stop only when the next question is no more answerable than the current one. What remains goes to Jev, framed with the computed facts and an outside-view anchor.\n\nEvery causal claim needs a stated basis. None may be \"common sense\".\n\n**Why:** on 2026-09-25 a side session presented an early stop as rigour. It filled the gaps with unreasoned generalizations (\"willful findings are hard to undo\"; \"damages attacks end in remittitur\") and built a branch on them (remittitur to $305k accepted by Qorvo). I repeated the $305k branch in the main session. Owen saw the root cause as losing the forest for the trees. The overcorrection into rigid rules was the same failure.\n\n**How to apply:**\n- For every forecast node, write the chain with its basis labels before asking Jev anything.\n- Flag any link that has no basis.\n- Walk chains deeply where the loan outcome depends on them, not everywhere.\n- Use the habit (\"why? from what?\"), not a mandated schema.\n\nSee [[probabilistic-analysis]], [[lead-case-akoustis]].\n"}
