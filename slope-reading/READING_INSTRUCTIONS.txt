Purpose
Recover how Slope's work develops through the assigned history. We want to understand what the work is for, how the agent understands the situation, what shapes its choices, and how those choices affect what happens next. These readings will join into a continuous account from which meaningful measurements of a session's trajectory can be developed.

The reading
Read the supplied context and the assigned history in order, including full tool arguments and results. Follow the developing situation. What is the agent trying to accomplish here? What has it learned? What does it treat as important or worth doing next? How does its chosen work affect the intended outcome, the available options, and the remaining work?

Explain the connections that make the sequence intelligible. A discovery may change an assumption, open an alternative, or leave a prior approach intact. An instruction may change the desired outcome, restore an existing understanding, or affect a particular part of the work. A plan or delegated assignment can shape which questions receive attention. Trace how those changes carry into subsequent choices and results where the assigned history shows them.

Give ordinary work the same interpretive attention as corrections. Consider relevant alternatives or dependencies when they help explain the chosen course, including necessary work that never became an explicit task. Ground their relevance in the purpose and information available at that time. Concentration, persistence, preparation, and substantial changes in direction can each serve the work; understand their role in the situation.

Organizing understanding

Writing the account
Write trace.txt as a connected account in plain prose, organized around meaningful stretches of work. Explain what drove the course and what changed through it. Include source references alongside the passages that support the explanation. Use the amount of detail each stretch needs. Where the sources leave an important explanation unresolved, say what is unresolved and how it affects the account. A qualification belongs where it changes the interpretation of a choice, consequence, or relationship.

End with the situation at the boundary: the direction being pursued, what has become possible or settled, and the important threads another reading must pick up. The coordinator will connect these accounts across assignments.
